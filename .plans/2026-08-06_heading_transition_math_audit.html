Heading transition math audit

Projectbordeaux
Versionv2
Statusdone · 2026-08-06

Transitions into Manual or Targets mode were acquiring an inactive, path-wide interpolated heading instead of the next authored anchor, which produced a real heading reversal across every planner.

Context

The reported Tangent → Targets path approached a −45° target from an approximately 180° tangent. The rendered robot turned past the intended acquisition direction toward 0°, then reversed back toward −45°. A direct numeric reproduction confirmed that the oscillation existed in the planner's desired heading samples before playback interpolation or translation-priority following.

PlannerMeasured reversal before fixSource
Profiled spline10.959°Shared native planner
Optimized trajectory10.959°Shared native planner

LabVIEW clothoid10.873°Compatibility planner

Finding

Root cause: Manual and Targets anchors are interpolated across the entire path, even where another heading law is active. At the mode boundary, the continuity smoother used the first interpolated outgoing sample as its acquisition goal. If that hidden value lies on one side of the next authored target, the blend reaches it and the outgoing law immediately reverses toward the real target.

The timing-priority follower, angular constraints, playback interpolation, and transition placement controls were not the cause. The reversal was already present in the desired spatial heading law and was mirrored by the static browser planner.

Recommendation

When entering Manual or Targets mode, identify the first real pinned waypoint or rotation target within the contiguous outgoing law. Acquire that anchor directly on the shortest unwrapped arc with a minimum-jerk spatial blend. Complete the blend within the authored before/split/after distance, hold the acquired heading until the anchor, then resume ordinary anchor interpolation.

Invariant: A transition may change angular direction after reaching an authored anchor, but it must not invent an angular reversal before that anchor.

Implementation steps

  1. Add shared hard-anchor goal resolution and monotonic acquisition to src/shared/planners/headingTransitions.ts.
  2. Supply Manual and Targets anchors from both the native PM planner and LabVIEW-compatible timeline builder.
  3. Mirror the same algorithm in the canonical static browser renderer.
  4. Add direct minimum-jerk, four-planner, and live browser-preview regression coverage.
