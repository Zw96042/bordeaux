# Edit notes

## Master

- Timeline: 1920×1080, 30 fps for the supplied draft; capture the product at 60 fps so speed ramps remain clean.
- Color: Rec.709, legal range, graphite blacks above 0 IRE so interface detail survives compression.
- Deliverables: 3840×2160 ProRes 422 HQ master, 1920×1080 H.264 web export, 1080×1920 vertical cut, 1080×1080 square cut.
- Keep the wine-glass mark vector-sharp. Do not apply bloom to ivory type.

## Motion

- Identity cards: 2–2.5% camera push over the full shot.
- Product capture: prioritize legibility over constant motion. One pointer action at a time.
- Transitions: 6–10 frame graphite dissolves or direct cuts on voiceover beats. No whip pans, glitch, or generic zoom transitions. The authored wine-lens cut belongs only inside the identity opener; do not reuse it between product chapters.
- Opener: `render-videos.sh` uses the 60 fps **Spill / Route** master when present and holds its final frame to the existing four-second edit point. Use **Precision Lock** when the cut needs a faster identity beat; **Ribbon Flight** is the editorial campaign alternative.

## Replacement workflow

The supplied `bordeaux-launch-draft.mp4` uses a code-built Plan visual so the cut is immediately reviewable. Record shots `03`–`10`, align them to the same timecodes, then replace that section without changing title/end-card timings.

## Safety and truthfulness

Keep “beta” visible on the end card and in the upload description. Do not show a robot moving near people, imply globally optimal planning, imply automatic application of agent proposals, or show Bordeaux deploying robot code.
