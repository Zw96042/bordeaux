# Audio brief

## Voice

Warm, direct, and technically confident; 145–155 words per minute. Avoid trailer voice, irony, or exaggerated urgency. Record 24-bit / 48 kHz in a dry room, 15–20 cm from the microphone, with two full takes and isolated pickups for “Aquitaine,” “Bordeaux,” and “versioned handoff.”

## Music

Minimal 92–100 BPM bed in D minor or F major: felt piano or soft mallet pulse, low restrained synth, and one warm harmonic lift at 00:24.5. No corporate ukulele, epic percussion, vineyard clichés, or busy arpeggios that compete with interface detail.

## Sound design

- 00:00: soft glass resonance, not a sharp clink
- 00:04: low tactile chapter thump
- 00:06.5–00:24.5: quiet path ticks at waypoints and a subtle timeline movement layer
- 00:24.5: three soft connection clicks for routine nodes
- 00:28.5: dry validation chime
- 00:32.5: return of the glass resonance, resolved

## Mix targets

- Integrated loudness: approximately −14 LUFS for web
- True peak: no higher than −1 dBTP
- Voice: consistently 4–6 dB above the music bed
- Deliver full mix, music-only, effects-only, and clean voice stems
